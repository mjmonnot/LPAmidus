**Author:** Matthew J. Monnot (Vocentive) · ORCID [0000-0002-9489-1777](https://orcid.org/0000-0002-9489-1777) · matt@vocentive.com

## What this repository is

A **limited public companion** to the preprint: citation metadata, data-access pointers, and a paper-facing construct → MIDUS variable map.

It is **not** a full replication package. Analysis code, derived person-level files, exploratory model archives, and internal research notes are maintained privately and are available **on reasonable request** for verification purposes (and may be posted more fully after peer review).

## Study in brief

Secondary analysis of the MIDUS national panel (core *N* = 7,108 across roughly two decades) and an independent Refresher sample (*N* = 3,577). Latent personality profiles were enumerated from Big Five and Agency indicators; a joint latent Markov model described profile transitions. Study 1 addressed profile structure, replication, and incremental validity; Study 2 tested hypotheses on transition pathways, skill-related criteria, and self-determination theory predictors of transition direction.

The study was **not preregistered**.
